# Company logos

Companies are included based on Hacktech's historical relationships provided by the directors. These are not claims of sponsorship for the upcoming event.

- `github.svg`: official white lockup, [GitHub Brand Toolkit](https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo), downloaded from https://brand.github.com/GitHub_Logos.zip.
- `drw.svg`: white logo from the header of https://www.drw.com/, with the accent set to gray.
- `mlh.svg`: official white logo, [MLH Brand Guidelines](https://www.mlh.com/brand-guidelines), downloaded from https://static.mlh.io/brand-assets/logo/official/mlh-logo-white.svg. This is the general logo, not a season membership badge.

- `openai.svg`: [OpenAI wordmark SVG](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:OpenAI_logo_2025_(wordmark).svg), authored by OpenAI and sourced from https://openai.com/brand/. Black fill changed to white; vector paths preserved.

The remaining PNGs are prepared from the existing past Hacktech sponsor assets in `images/`. Their white canvas is removed at the original resolution, retaining continuous alpha coverage at edges. Artwork is white, with gray accents for Cubist. Blank margins are trimmed and copies are exported at 600px wide before normal browser scaling. Original files are preserved. No runtime SVG or CSS color filters are used.
